I am an AI Product Designer and UX researcher based in San Francisco, with a BFA in User Experience Design from the Savannah College of Art and Design. My work sits where product thinking, visual craft, and human behavior meet.

I have designed across a wide range of contexts: the Lumi and Wonderbudi social robots, the Legaleey legal search platform, the Explorius travel app, the Baloo storytelling toy, and the six Explorations tools and brand systems.

At SCAD I completed a graduate studio on the complexity of simplicity, and it still shapes how I open every ambiguous brief: find the smallest unit of user value, then build outward from there.

I do not stop at the mockup. The tools on my Explorations page, from a local AI research assistant to a browser only insurance appeal kit, were designed, coded, tested, and shipped by me end to end. Across Legaleey and those tools, the through line is making AI behavior legible and trustworthy to the people using it.

I am available for full time roles and select freelance collaborations. If you are building something that needs to be both rigorously designed and genuinely human, I would love to talk.
