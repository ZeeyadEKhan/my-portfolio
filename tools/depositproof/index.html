MODULE 02

Wear or damage

Landlords cannot deduct for normal wear and tear in any state. The line between wear and damage decides most deposit disputes. Pick what your landlord charged for and see where it usually falls.

Check a deduction

Tenancy length matters. Courts expect more wear after three years than after six months, and they prorate the useful life of paint and carpet.

Usually normal wear

Small nail holes in walls

Courts in most states treat a reasonable number of small nail holes as ordinary use of the home. A charge to patch them is usually not deductible.

General principle across state deposit statutes. Verify your statute before you rely on it.
